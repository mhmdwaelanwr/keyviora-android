## Product UX priorities
1. Fast one-handed access to **Lock**, **Volume**, and **Power**.
2. Dangerous shutdown always requires a warning first.
3. Resizable floating control with high-contrast, large tap targets.
4. No ads, trackers, network dependency, or unrelated permissions.
5. Android Accessibility only for overlay/global system actions.
